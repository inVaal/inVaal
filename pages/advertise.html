Our Approach

Advertising should create value.

InVaal [016] is being developed around the idea that advertising can support both businesses and the community when it is relevant, transparent and useful.

Relevance

Promotional content should have a clear connection to the people and communities InVaal [016] serves.

Transparency

Paid promotional arrangements should not be presented in a way that deliberately misleads users about their nature.

Community Value

We want advertising to help people discover useful businesses, services, products, events and opportunities.

Responsible Promotion

Promotional material should comply with applicable laws and should not intentionally promote fraudulent, harmful or misleading activity.

Current Status

Let's build the advertising model together.

InVaal [016] is currently an independent, developing community platform and is not currently registered as a formal business entity. Advertising packages, pricing, placements and partnership arrangements should therefore be discussed and confirmed directly with InVaal [016].
