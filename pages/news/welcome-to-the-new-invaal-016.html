A new chapter begins for local information, discovery and community connection across the Vaal.

Published by InVaal [016] • 3 September 2026

Welcome to InVaal [016] — a growing community platform created with one simple idea: local information should be easier to find, understand and share.

The Vaal is home to businesses, entrepreneurs, creatives, organisations, community initiatives, events and people doing meaningful things every day. Yet finding the right local information can often mean searching across different platforms, social media pages and websites.

InVaal [016] is being developed to bring more of that information together in one place.

The idea

What is InVaal [016]?

InVaal [016] is a community-focused digital platform for the Vaal Triangle and surrounding communities.

The platform is being developed around the idea of connecting people with useful local information — whether they are looking for a business, discovering a creative, finding an opportunity, attending an event or simply trying to understand what is happening around them.

Local information. Local people. Local opportunities.

One place to discover more of what the Vaal has to offer.

Why we're building it

Built around the community.

InVaal [016] is not intended to replace the organisations, businesses, publications or community groups already serving the Vaal.

Instead, the goal is to help people discover them.

A local business should have a chance to be discovered. A creative should have somewhere to showcase their work. A community project should be easier to find. And a useful opportunity should not disappear simply because someone missed the original post.

InVaal [016] is being built around that idea.

This is where you come in

InVaal [016] grows with the community.

The platform can only become useful if the community helps make it useful.

Businesses, creatives, organisations, event organisers and community members can help by sharing information, suggesting stories, correcting outdated information and letting others know about what is happening locally.
